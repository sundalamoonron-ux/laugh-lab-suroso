# One-Liner Laugh Lab — Candidates

Event: `u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae` — "One-Liner Laugh Lab", $2.50, top 8 paid.
Prompt: the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock.
Gate: UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.
Judge criteria (in order): (1) funny one-liner answering the prompt, (2) funniness — twist/deadpan turn that lands, (3) originality — fresh angle, not the first excuse anyone would think of, (4) fit to the prompt, (5) tightness — short and punchy.
